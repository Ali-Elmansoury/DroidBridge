# DroidBridge — Commercial Licensing

DroidBridge is free for personal, non-commercial use. Commercial use requires
a paid license.

## What counts as commercial use?

- Selling DroidBridge or a modified version of it
- Bundling DroidBridge into a paid product or service
- Using DroidBridge internally at a company or organization
- Deploying DroidBridge as part of a paid support workflow
- Any use that generates revenue, directly or indirectly

## License tiers

## How to get a commercial license

Email **ali.elmansoury21@gmail.com** with:

1. Your name and company/organization

## What a commercial license includes

- Right to use DroidBridge for commercial purposes
- All future updates within the licensed version (1.x)
- Email support for setup and integration questions
- Your organization named in the commercial license record
